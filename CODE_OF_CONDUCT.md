# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Adun is a small open-source project, and we want taking part in it to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience level or the language they speak. Contributors write in Romanian and English, and either is welcome in issues and pull requests.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their first.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and take feedback the same way.
- Stay on topic. Issues and pull requests are for work on Adun.
- Respect people's privacy. Do not share someone's personal information, and do not post real financial data, backups or screenshots of real accounts.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments, including about someone's gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, age, ethnicity, nationality, religion or experience level
- Threats or intimidation
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic or promotional posts
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run for the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer at **contact@adun.ro**. Please include links or screenshots where you can, and what happened. Reports are treated confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will respond to reports promptly and fairly. Depending on the severity and whether it is repeated, the response may be:

1. A private reminder of the expectations, with an explanation
2. A public or private warning
3. Removal or editing of comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions
4. A temporary block from the project's spaces
5. A permanent ban

Maintainers who do not follow this Code of Conduct may face the same consequences. Anyone affected by a decision can reply to the person who sent it to ask for a second look.
